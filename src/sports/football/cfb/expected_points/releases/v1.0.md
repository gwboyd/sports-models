# Initial production baseline

## Public Summary

The CFB expected-points model predicts each team's score from recent efficiency, explosiveness, pregame strength, game context, and sportsbook consensus. It converts those scores into spread and total picks, estimates confidence independently for each market, and shops corroborated sportsbook quotes for the line used to make and grade the pick.

## Changes

- Includes games when at least one participant is FBS and requires at least one current spread quote and one current total quote from a real sportsbook before predicting the game.
- Builds leakage-safe, lagged team form from dynamically windowed exponential averages of offensive and defensive PPA, plus offensive and defensive explosiveness, pregame Elo, conference-game status, weekday, and implied team points.
- Uses a gradient-boosted score model to predict each team's expected points, then derives the projected spread and total from those scores.
- Defines the reference spread and total as the median of real sportsbook quotes, chooses direction against that consensus, and shops the execution line within the largest provider cluster spanning no more than two points.
- Keeps a market visible and trainable with one real quote, but requires at least two corroborating providers before it can become a lock. Independent spread and total confidence models use the execution-line edge and rank no more than five locks per market above the 55% probability and 0.5-point edge thresholds.
- Refuses to create a pick at or after kickoff, preserves picks once they enter the 30-minute lock window, and atomically records update history, current picks, and newly graded results.

## Evaluation

Completed games are ordered chronologically. The newest 20% form an outer score holdout, score parameters are selected with one train-before-validation split inside the older data, and the production score model is refit on all completed games. The confidence models train from the outer out-of-time score predictions, tune for log loss on their own chronological split, and then refit on that full holdout.

Opening-line, line-movement, market-depth, and no-vig moneyline feature groups are evaluated outside production. None are included in version 1.0 because they have not cleared the promotion gate: at least a 1% pooled Brier-score improvement, no worse log loss, and no evaluated season more than 2% worse on Brier score.

## Internal Notes

Version 1.0 is the bootstrap baseline introduced after the model was already operating. Pre-versioning picks, results, and update runs are attributed to 1.0 because their exact recipe version and source Git SHA were not recorded. This document describes the production recipe in place when version tracking began; it does not claim that every historical row attributed to 1.0 was generated by an identical code revision.

This baseline does not include player-level injury features or an explicit opponent-strength adjustment layer for the team efficiency features.
