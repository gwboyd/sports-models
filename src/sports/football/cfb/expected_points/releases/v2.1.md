# CFB Expected Points 2.1

## Public Summary

Keep expected-score predictions unchanged while replacing overstated betting confidence with conservative probabilities and a more useful weekly Lock selection policy.

## Changes

- Estimate spread win probabilities from unseen-game score errors, with strong shrinkage toward an even chance.
- Rank Locks by expected profit at standard -110 pricing, accounting for pushes and requiring at least a 52.5% resolved-win probability.
- Normally select up to three combined Locks: currently all spreads. Stronger candidates at 55% or higher can exceed those limits, even more than five in a week.
- Use a shrunken historical rate for total-pick probabilities without issuing total Locks.
- Aim for useful weekly selections without forcing a quota. Thin slates can have fewer or no Locks, and saved picks near kickoff retain their original status. Sportsbook corroboration requirements remain unchanged.
