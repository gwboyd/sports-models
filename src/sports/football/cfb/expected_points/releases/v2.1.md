# CFB Expected Points 2.1

## Public Summary

Keep expected-score predictions unchanged while replacing overstated betting confidence with conservative probabilities and a more useful weekly Lock selection policy.

## Changes

- Replace the spread win/loss classifier with an empirical absolute-error distribution fitted to chronological, out-of-time margin predictions. Win probability is `q = 0.5 + 0.1 × F(e)`, where `e` is execution-line edge and `F(e)` is the fraction of absolute errors smaller than that edge—an 80% discount of the symmetric-error advantage. Total probabilities use a shrunken historical hit rate; total Locks are disabled.
- Estimate pushes separately and rank eligible spreads by positive expected profit at assumed -110 pricing, requiring at least 52.5% resolved-win probability. Normally select up to three combined weekly Locks, currently all spreads; the requirement for two corroborating sportsbooks is unchanged.
- Allow spreads at 55% or higher to exceed the normal limit, including more than five Locks when warranted; never force a minimum. Preserve saved picks near kickoff, including their original version and Lock status, and count those Locks toward weekly capacity.
- Evaluation versus 2.0: the 2023–2025 weekly backtest on frozen historical inputs at assumed -110 returned +20.82 units across 130 Locks versus -9.36 across 399. The candidate's 95% interval spans -0.09 to +40.18 units; these seasons were reused during method selection, so the result does not establish future profitability.
