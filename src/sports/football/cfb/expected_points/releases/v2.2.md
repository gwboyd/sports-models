# More reliable score predictions

## Public Summary

Ground expected scores in market expectations and recent team performance, with stricter use of completed games and conservative confidence for both spread and total picks.

## Changes

- Predict team scores with ridge regression anchored to market-implied points, selecting the penalty and team-feature weight through chronological validation. Use smoothed PPA, success rate and explosiveness with half-strength ridge opponent adjustment, alongside pregame Elo ratings that follow the team being predicted. Ignore invalid nonpositive sportsbook totals.
- Train betting confidence from historical score forecasts refreshed as earlier games finish, shrinking error-based estimates 80% toward 50%. Freeze team-strength inputs before each week's first kickoff and allow eight hours for earlier results. Keep unfinished scores out of training, team ratings, efficiency history and grading.
- Select three supported spread and total Locks combined each week, then add picks at or above 57% estimated win probability, up to ten. The first three may fall below estimated break-even without increasing confidence; adverse execution edges and unsupported markets remain ineligible. Fewer selections are possible when support or training history is insufficient. An exact projected-total tie chooses Over before normal line shopping, without inventing an edge or extra confidence.
