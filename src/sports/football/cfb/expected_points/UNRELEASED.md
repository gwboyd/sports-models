# CFB Expected Points 2.0

## Public Summary

Account for opponent strength, broaden the measures of team efficiency, and learn from more seasons when predicting college football scores.

## Changes

- Adjust offensive and defensive performance for opponent strength, giving more credit for success against difficult opponents and less against weaker opponents. Blend observed and adjusted performance equally.
- Add predicted points added and success rate alongside explosiveness to capture both the value and consistency of a team's plays.
- Assess FBS opponents individually and use a shared FCS strength estimate where individual team data is limited.
- Expand training history to begin in 2018, with an additional season of background efficiency data. Exclude the 2020 COVID season from training and team-strength calculations.
