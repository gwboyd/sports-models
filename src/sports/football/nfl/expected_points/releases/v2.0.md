# NFL Expected Points 2.0

## Public Summary

Account for opponent strength when assessing team performance and predicting NFL scores.

## Changes

- Adjust offensive and defensive efficiency for the opponents faced, giving more credit for strong performances against difficult opponents and less against weaker opponents.
- Apply these adjustments to expected points added and success rate, including passing and rushing performance.
- Blend observed and opponent-adjusted performance equally to keep the influence of opponent estimates moderate.
- Assess starting quarterbacks using only their earlier starts, leaving unavailable history unknown.
