# Updated score predictions and weekly Locks

## Public Summary

Predict team scores from football analytics and optional sportsbook inputs, with stricter use of information available before kickoff and a clear weekly Lock selection rule.

## Changes

- Predict actual team points directly with ridge regression and optional sportsbook features, selecting the penalty and team-feature weight through chronological validation. Use smoothed EPA and success rate with half-strength ridge opponent adjustment. Exclude starting-quarterback ratings derived from game participation and game-observed roof status from prediction inputs.
- Train betting confidence from historical score forecasts refreshed as earlier games finish. Apply an eight-hour result-availability allowance to training, validation, opponent ratings and team-efficiency history, keeping unavailable results out of later predictions.
- Highlight the top three supported spread and total picks combined each week, ranked by estimated win probability. Keep those selections eligible below estimated break-even without increasing confidence; fewer are possible when support or training history is insufficient. An exact projected-total tie produces an Over pick without adding model edge or confidence.
