# NFL Expected Points 2.1

## Public Summary

Keep expected-score predictions unchanged while replacing overstated betting confidence with conservative probabilities and a more useful weekly Lock selection policy.

## Changes

- Replace win/loss classifiers with empirical absolute-error distributions fitted to chronological, out-of-time margin and total predictions. Win probability is `q = 0.5 + 0.1 × F(e)`, where `e` is execution-line edge and `F(e)` is the fraction of absolute errors smaller than that edge. This discounts the symmetric-error advantage by 80%, keeping estimates between 50% and 60%.
- Estimate pushes separately and rank eligible bets by positive expected profit at assumed -110 pricing, requiring at least 52.5% resolved-win probability. Apply one combined weekly limit of three Locks, normally up to two spreads and one total.
- Allow candidates at 55% or higher to exceed the normal market and combined limits, including more than five Locks when warranted; never force a minimum. Preserve saved picks near kickoff, including their original version and Lock status, and count those Locks toward weekly capacity.
- Evaluation versus 2.0: the 2023–2025 weekly backtest at assumed -110 returned +2.18 units across 164 Locks versus -19.73 across 461. The candidate's 95% interval spans -18.73 to +23.91 units; these seasons were reused during method selection, so the result does not establish future profitability.
